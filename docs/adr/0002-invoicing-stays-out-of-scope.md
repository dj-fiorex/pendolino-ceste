# Invoicing stays out of scope

The app knows every Cliente, every Campagna and every kilogram of Ceste that moved, so it looks one small step away from producing the milling invoice — and the mill already collects full tax details at milling time precisely to invoice. It still stays out.

Tracking Ceste needs only a name to be useful, and it needs it in seconds at a busy counter; invoicing needs complete and correct fiscal data, and pulls in tax rules, credit notes and an existing accounting system the mill already uses. Folding it in would make the fast path at the counter answer to the slow path's data requirements. The app records what it knows and stops there.
