# Nothing that happened is ever deleted

This app exists to reconstruct where Ceste went and who was holding them. Every delete is a hole in that reconstruction, and holes appear precisely where somebody had a reason to want one. So no record standing for something that actually happened is ever removed.

The rule has already been reached independently three times. A Cliente that disappears from the Gestionale is marked inactive rather than deleted, because an archived billing record says nothing about whether that person is currently holding three Ceste. An Operatore who leaves after the Campagna is deactivated, and the Movimenti they registered stay attributed to them and stay readable. A Cesta broken at the mill or lost to a Cliente becomes Dismessa through a Rettifica that records what became of it — the mill owner asked for exactly this, unprompted: *"sarebbe opportuno sapere che fine ha fatto la cesta"*.

Everywhere, deactivating means *no longer in play*. It never means *did not happen*.

## Consequences

Every read that populates a picker, a search or a count has to filter on active status, and forgetting to do so surfaces retired Ceste and departed Operatori in places they do not belong. That cost is paid in a great many small places, in exchange for never having to explain a gap in the history of a Cesta that somebody swears they returned.

It also makes the reason on a Rettifica mandatory rather than optional. A Rettifica with no reason is a delete wearing a different hat, and losing a Cesta to a Cliente is a different fact from breaking one at the mill.
